      <p class="section-number">05</p>

      <div>
        <h2>Working Approach</h2>
        <p>
          A technical and analytical approach to commercial problems.
        </p>
      </div>

    </div>


    <div class="project-principles">

      <div>
        <strong>Understand the system</strong>
        <p>
          Start with the technical, industrial and market fundamentals rather than assumptions.
        </p>
      </div>

      <div>
        <strong>Measure where possible</strong>
        <p>
          Use data, specifications, performance indicators and financial parameters to support decisions.
        </p>
      </div>

      <div>
        <strong>Connect technical and commercial realities</strong>
        <p>
          A technically attractive solution must also work within supply, logistics,
          market and financial constraints.
        </p>
      </div>

      <div>
        <strong>Build for execution</strong>
        <p>
          Strategy is useful only when it can be translated into suppliers,
          contracts, operations and measurable results.
        </p>
      </div>
