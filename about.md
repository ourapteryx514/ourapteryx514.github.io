        <div class="timeline-marker"></div>

        <div class="timeline-meta">

        </div>

        <div class="timeline-content">

          <h3>Optical Parametric Oscillators & Doctoral Research</h3>

          <p>
            At ICFO, I worked with the
            <strong>Optical Parametric Oscillators</strong> group,
            focusing on ultrafast laser systems, nonlinear frequency conversion
            and optical parametric oscillators.
          </p>

          <p>
            I subsequently continued my doctoral research and studies there.
            My PhD research focused on
            <strong>high-power ultrafast optical parametric oscillators
            from the visible to the mid-infrared</strong>,
            including femtosecond and picosecond systems,
            high-repetition-rate sources and nonlinear frequency conversion.
          </p>

        </div>

      </div>


      <div class="timeline-item">

        <div class="timeline-marker"></div>

          <p class="timeline-location">
            Austin, Texas, USA
          </p>

          <p>
            After completing the research and study phase of my PhD at ICFO,
            I moved to the United States and continued my academic and research
            activities at the <strong>University of Texas at Austin</strong>,
            working in optics and photonics.
          </p>

        </div>

      </div>

    </div>


    <div class="about-highlight">

      <p class="highlight-label">Scientific work</p>

      <p>
        My research has involved nonlinear optical materials such as
        <strong>PPLN and BIBO</strong>, synchronously pumped optical parametric
        oscillators, fiber-laser-pumped sources, high-repetition-rate systems,
        harmonic generation and ultrafast photonics.
      </p>

    </div>
