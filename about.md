# ABOUT ME

I am a physicist with a background in **photonics, ultrafast lasers and nonlinear optics**.

My academic work has focused on optical parametric oscillators, nonlinear frequency conversion and ultrafast laser systems, while my professional interests have gradually expanded into **industrial technology, energy, polymers, international trade and technical consulting**.

I am particularly interested in work that connects scientific thinking with practical industrial problems.

## Background

I completed doctoral research in physics and photonics, working on high-power ultrafast optical parametric oscillators operating from the visible to the mid-infrared.

Over the years, I have also been involved in industrial and commercial activities related to chemicals, polymers, international sourcing, logistics and technology.

## Areas of Interest

- Photonics and laser technology
- Nonlinear optics
- Solar energy and photovoltaic systems
- Industrial technology
- Polymers and chemical industries
- Energy systems
- International trade and technical consulting
- Science, history and technology

## This Website

I use this website to document my research, technical projects, professional interests and personal observations.
