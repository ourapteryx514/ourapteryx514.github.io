My research background is in **photonics, ultrafast lasers, and nonlinear optics**, with particular focus on optical parametric oscillators and frequency conversion from the visible to the mid-infrared.

## Doctoral Research

**PhD in Physics – Photonics**

My doctoral research focused on:

**High-power ultrafast optical parametric oscillators from the visible to mid-infrared**

The work involved the development and characterization of femtosecond and picosecond synchronously pumped optical parametric oscillators, covering repetition rates from tens of MHz up to the GHz regime.

Key areas included:

- Ultrafast optical parametric oscillators
- Visible, near-infrared and mid-infrared generation
- Nonlinear frequency conversion
- High-repetition-rate laser systems
- Synchronous optical cavities
- Dispersion management
- Harmonic generation

## Experimental Experience

My research has involved nonlinear optical materials including:

**PPLN · MgO:PPLN · BIBO**

and laser platforms including:

**Ti:sapphire lasers · Yb-based fiber lasers**

I have also worked on cavity design, dispersion compensation, harmonic generation and synchronization of ultrafast optical systems.
